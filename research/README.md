# Active research

Open investigations and ongoing search topics. Unlike `paper/theory/` (stable
explanatory docs) and `generated/` (regenerable artifacts), everything here is
*in flight* — claims may be provisional, and items graduate out as they close.

- **[PENDING_TASKS.md](PENDING_TASKS.md)** — the cross-cutting task list.
- **[DISCOVERIES_PENDING_ANALYSIS.md](DISCOVERIES_PENDING_ANALYSIS.md)** — provisional findings not yet digested into the catalog.
- **[FUTURE_WORK.md](FUTURE_WORK.md)** — longer-horizon directions.

## Topics (one folder per large open item)

- **[small-rank-search/](small-rank-search/)** — direct search for a *fixed small*
  shape's minimal rank (SAT / ALS / enumeration). E.g. ⟨3,3,3⟩ at 22→21 muls,
  rank-5 walkthroughs. **Distinct** from combining schemes to cover ⟨n,m,p⟩.
- **[hopcroft-kerr-2np/](hopcroft-kerr-2np/)** — constructive methods for ⟨2,n,p⟩
  and the bridge-3 impossibility question.
- **[serendipity/](serendipity/)** — serendipitous (bud-fusing) products and a
  proper *savings-formula* bud score.
- **[projection/](projection/)** — projection closure and the projection-margin
  (μ) score, incl. the linear-projection gap vs FMM.
- **[trilinear-aggregation/](trilinear-aggregation/)** — DIS09 / Pan TA
  reproduction.
