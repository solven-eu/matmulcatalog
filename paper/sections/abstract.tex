The 2022--2026 burst of activity in small-format matrix multiplication
(AlphaTensor 2022, AlphaEvolve 2025, Schwartz--Zwecher 2025, and
Perminov's open-source flip-graph
framework~\cite{perminov2026fast,perminov2025fast}) has produced
striking results but scattered them across fields, attribution
conventions, and serialisation formats.
We present a unified, machine-checkable catalog covering shapes up to
\nmpshape{32}{32}{32} over \Rationals, \Integers, \Reals, \Complex,
\Ftwo, and \Fthree, with a separate axis for commutative algorithms
(Waksman 1970, Makarov 1986, Rosowski 2019). Derivation over the
catalog uses a \emph{frontier-closure search} that recombines
entries by axis-flip, Kronecker, axis concatenation,
serendipitous products, recombination-with-allocation (with optional
output peeling and leaf-level pair fusion), and downward projection.

A central methodological point is the \emph{non-overlap property}: our
composition cannot rediscover the shared bilinear products that
hand-crafted constructions are built around --- a clean
line between ``find a cleverer bilinear core'' and ``compose known
cores'' that resolves several attribution puzzles. Empirically, over
\Rationals (2026-07-12 snapshot), our derived schemes strictly beat
every external holding on 908 shapes and trail on six audited ones ---
all six explained by a single sharing device (fusion kernels) that our
composition deliberately forgoes.

We refresh the DIS09 comparison tables, split per field with a
commutative column, and provide tooling to regenerate them as the
catalog evolves.
